the studio

Built by one.
For many.

Unit 17 is a one-person indie studio based in Ottawa, Canada. Founded by Arjun Arun — a security professional, car enthusiast, and dad of two — with a simple belief: great software doesn't need a big team. It needs the right person, obsessing over the right problem.

The name comes from the people that matter most. Every digit in 17 is someone in this family. That's not a gimmick — it's a reminder of who we're building for.

From Kerala to Al Ain to Ottawa — a long road that shaped a very particular taste for things that are precise, purposeful, and a little unexpected.

how we work

Our principles.

Not a manifesto. Just how we think about building things.

01
Ship it.

A good product in people's hands beats a perfect product that never ships. We move fast, release early, and iterate relentlessly based on real feedback.

02
Solve real problems.

Every product starts with a genuine frustration — something we actually felt. We don't build features. We solve problems people actually have.

03
Design is not decoration.

How something works and how it looks are the same thing. Every interaction is intentional. Nothing is added without a reason to be there.

04
Stay small on purpose.

Small means fast. Small means honest. Small means every decision matters and every user is a real person — not a DAU metric.
